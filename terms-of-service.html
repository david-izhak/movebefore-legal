1. What Move Before does

Move Before pauses the apps you choose and asks you to do a short exercise before you open them. After the exercise the app opens for a limited time and then is paused again. The app uses Android permissions (Accessibility Service, Usage Access, Display over other apps) to notice when a chosen app opens and to show the pause screen.

2. Free version and Premium

  • The free version locks one app, and after an exercise the app stays open for 10 minutes. Exercises, streaks, Insights and Emergency Unlock are the same in the free version and in Premium.
  • Premium lets you lock as many apps as you need and choose how long an app stays open after an exercise, from 3 to 30 minutes.
  • If Premium ends, nothing is deleted: the app you added first stays locked, the others wait in your Lock List, and your chosen time is kept, until Premium is active again. Until then an app stays open for 10 minutes after an exercise.
