Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Move Before app ("Move Before", "the app", "we") handles, where it is kept and how it is used. Move Before is developed by David Izhak.

1. Where your information is kept

Move Before has no servers and no user accounts. What you enter and what the app records about your activity is stored on your device, in the app's private storage, and we have no access to it. Apart from purchases, which Google Play handles (section 8), and Android backups (section 9), information is sent off your device only in the cases described in section 5 "Advertising" and section 6 "Usage and crash reports", and only as described there. What is never sent is listed in section 4.

2. What the app keeps on your device, and why

The app keeps the kinds of information below. Each is used only for what is described next to it.

  • Your profile — your name, your age and, if you choose to give it, your gender. Your age, together with your daily phone time, is used for the time estimate shown during setup. Your age also decides whether the app shows ads and asks about usage and crash reports: it does neither if you are under 18. Your name and gender are kept as part of your profile, which you can see and change in Settings; the current version of the app does not use them for anything else.
  • Your setup answers — how active you are, how many hours a day you spend on your phone, the things you notice lately (for example "Poor sleep" or "Back pain") and the goals you pick. They set the starting exercise difficulty and suggest goals; your goals are shown on the pause screen and in Insights, and Insights compares your answer about phone time with your actual time on the phone.
  • Your Lock List — the apps you chose to lock and whether protection is on, so the app knows which apps to pause.
  • Your activity in Move Before — the exercises you complete and their details (such as which exercise and when); each time the pause screen appears, for which app, when, and what you chose there (an exercise, "I changed my mind" or Emergency Unlock) or that you left it without choosing; and your streak. Shown on the Home and Insights screens.
  • Your time in locked apps — for the apps you lock: the minutes you spend in them each day, the time a day you usually spent in each one before you locked it, the time saved on each day since, and how long you spent in an app during each unlock you earned with an exercise. Shown in Insights. Nothing is kept about the time you spend in apps you have never locked.
  • Settings and app state — your settings and choices in the app, including your answer about usage and crash reports, whether Premium is active, an unlock that is running, and the records the app needs to keep protection working (for example, which turned-off permission it has already told you about).

None of this information is used for ads. Of it, only what section 6 describes is sent, as usage and crash reports, and only if you allow it; nothing listed in section 4 is ever sent.

3. Permissions and what they let the app see

  • Accessibility Service — lets Move Before notice which app comes to the screen (the app's package name), so it can pause the apps you chose. It does not read what is on the screen: no messages, no passwords, nothing you type. This information is used at that moment and is not stored.
  • Usage Access — gives Move Before Android's usage records: which apps you used, when and for how long, and when you unlocked your phone. Move Before uses them to:
    • see which app is in front, so it can pause the apps you chose;
    • show you how you use your phone — for example, the time in each app over the last 7 days, to sort the apps you can lock during setup and in Lock List; and in Insights, the time in your locked apps and when you last used each of them, your total time on the phone a day and how many times a day you unlock it.
    Of these records, the app keeps only your time in locked apps described in section 2. Everything else is worked out on your device when a screen needs it and is not kept.
  • List of installed apps — to show the apps you can lock, and to recognise your home screen, which is not counted in your time on the phone. Only the apps you choose are kept.
  • Display over other apps — to show the pause screen on top of a locked app.
  • Notifications (optional) — to show that protection is on, how much unlocked time is left, and when a permission Move Before needs is turned off.
  • Running in the background and after a restart — to keep protection working.
  • Internet — to talk to Google Play about purchases and subscription status; in the free version, to show ads and to ask Google about your ad choices (section 5); and, if you allow it, to send usage and crash reports (section 6).
  • Advertising ID (free version) — lets Google AdMob read your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads (section 5). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

You can turn Accessibility Service, Usage Access, Display over other apps and notifications off at any time in Android Settings. Without Accessibility Service, Usage Access or Display over other apps, Move Before cannot pause apps.

4. What is never sent from your device

Whatever you choose, Move Before never sends the following to us, to Google or to anyone else, and never gives it to the advertising or analytics tools in the app:

  • which apps are installed on your device, which apps you use and for how long — this includes everything Move Before learns through Accessibility Service and Usage Access, your Lock List and the names of apps in any form;
  • your answers to "What do you notice lately?";
  • your name.

It can leave your device only inside Android's backup of your device, in your own Google account (section 9).

5. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads: a banner at the top of the Home and Insights screens and one ad card in Insights, each marked as an ad. Premium has no ads. Ads are never shown on the pause, exercise or success screens, during setup, in Lock List or Settings, or over other apps. Ads are limited to those suitable for teens and older. If the age in your Move Before profile is under 18, the app shows no ads and does not contact Google about ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The advertising tools in the app start only when an ad may be shown. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects information from your device: your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which your approximate location can be estimated), how you interact with the ads and the app (for example, app launches and taps), and information about how the app performs (for example, its launch time). Google uses this information as described in Google's Privacy Policy and at "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

If you are 18 or older and use the free version, each time you open the app it asks Google whether a consent form is needed where you are (Google's User Messaging Platform); for this, Google receives your IP address and information about your device and the app. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's form asks for your consent first, and depending on your choice the ads are personalized, not personalized, or limited. In some US states, showing personalized ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information under local law; there, Google's form lets you opt out. Where Google provides it, you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

6. Usage and crash reports (only if you allow them)

If you are 18 or older, the app asks whether it may send usage and crash reports ("Help us improve Move Before"). If you allow it, Move Before sends the following to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), to find problems and improve the app:

  • which screens you open, and what you do on the pause and exercise screens — for example, that you started or completed an exercise and which one, used Emergency Unlock or chose "I changed my mind";
  • the steps of setup, when the subscription screen is shown, changes of the exercise difficulty, and turning protection on or off;
  • that a permission the app needs was turned off, and roughly how long protection was off;
  • your age range (for example, 25–34);
  • what Firebase records by itself: when the app is opened for the first time, sessions and how long the app is in use, updates of the app and of Android, removal of the app or of its data, and a Premium purchase (the plan, its price and currency, and whether it started with a free trial);
  • the app version, the device model, the Android version and language, and the approximate location (such as the country or region) that Google works out from your IP address;
  • crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, and information about the device.

This information is linked to a random installation ID, not to your name, your Google account or your advertising ID. What the app itself sends is fixed in the app, and nothing listed in section 4 is included. Google processes this information on our behalf; sharing it with other Google services is turned off.

If you do not allow it, or if you are under 18, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Usage and crash reports. Refusing does not change how the app works.

7. Security

The information on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which Android separates from other apps: other apps cannot read it. The app relies on this protection and on your device's own security, such as the screen lock and device encryption; it does not add encryption of its own. Information sent for ads and for usage and crash reports is sent over encrypted connections.

8. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. Your payment details are handled by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy; the app never sees them. The app keeps only whether Premium is active. If you allow usage reports, Firebase also records a purchase as described in section 6.

9. Backups

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the app's stored information in the backup of your device kept in your Google account. Such backups are made and managed by Google under its own terms; we have no access to them. You can manage or turn off backups in your Android and Google account settings.

10. Sharing

We do not sell your information for money, and we do not receive the information kept on your device. Information is sent only to Google: for ads in the free version (section 5) and, if you allow it, as usage and crash reports (section 6). How to opt out of personalized ads is described in section 5.

11. How long information is kept and how to delete it

Information stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. To delete it:

  • open Android Settings > Apps > Move Before > Storage and clear the app's storage, or
  • uninstall the app.

Copies in Android backups are managed in your Google account settings.

Usage reports are kept in Google Firebase for 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days; then they are deleted. To have the usage and crash reports linked to your installation deleted sooner, send us the Installation ID shown in Settings while reports are on (section 15). Information Google collects for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

12. Your choices

  • See what the app keeps about your activity on the Home, Lock List and Insights screens, and see or change your profile and goals in Settings.
  • Pause protection on the Home screen, remove apps from the Lock List, or turn permissions off in Android Settings.
  • Change your answer about usage and crash reports, and your ad choices where Google provides them, in Settings. Premium has no ads.
  • Delete everything as described in section 11.

Usage and crash reports are sent only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Where the law gives you rights over your information, such as access or deletion, contact us (section 15); you may also complain to your data protection authority. Because the information on your device does not reach us, we cannot view, correct or delete it for you.

13. Children

Move Before is not directed at children under 13, and the app asks for an age of 13 or older during setup. If the age in your profile is under 18, the app shows no ads, does not ask Google about ads and sends no usage or crash reports.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and at its public address, with a new "Last updated" date. If the app starts handling your information in a new way, the policy will say so before that happens.
